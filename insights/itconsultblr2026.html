Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is TDS applicable on payments to AWS, Google Cloud, or Microsoft Azure from an Indian company?

A: This is a frequently litigated question. CBDT's position is that cloud computing payments constitute Fees for Technical Services (FTS) or Royalties, attracting TDS at 20% (domestic rate) or lower DTAA rate. AWS and Azure are based in the USA — the India-USA DTAA FTS rate is 15%. The cloud provider must hold a permanent establishment in India for TDS to apply at the treaty rate. PDKA provides case-specific TDS analysis for cloud payments.

Q: What is the income tax rate for a private limited company in Bangalore?

A: Domestic companies in India have three main tax rate options: (1) Old regime — 30% base rate with all deductions; (2) Section 115BAA — 22% base rate (~25.17% effective) without most deductions; (3) Section 115BAB — 15% base rate (~17.01% effective) for new manufacturing companies only. Most Bangalore IT and service companies use 115BAA at 25.17% effective rate. The optional tax regime is irrevocable once exercised.
