The 15% Rate — India's Sharpest Manufacturing Incentive

Section 115BAB offers a 15% base corporate tax rate (effective 17.01% with surcharge and cess) for new domestic manufacturing companies — the lowest corporate tax rate available to any company in India, and competitive globally. At 17%, India's manufacturing tax rate matches Singapore, beats China (25%), and rivals Vietnam. Combined with PLI incentives and state subsidies, the effective post-incentive tax burden can fall below 5% in the first five years.

Who Qualifies?

  • Must be a domestic company (Indian incorporated) — WOS of foreign parent qualifies
  • Incorporated on or after October 1, 2019
  • Must commence manufacturing on or before March 31, 2025 (check Budget 2026 for any extension)
  • Not formed by splitting, reconstruction, or amalgamation of an existing business
  • Plant and machinery must be new — second-hand machinery cannot exceed 20% of total plant value
  • Business must be manufacturing or production only — cannot include trading, services, or financial activities

What You Lose Under 115BAB

  • No deductions under Chapter VI-A (80C, 80D, 80G, 80IC, etc.)
  • No Section 10AA deduction (SEZ benefit) — cannot combine SEZ and 115BAB
  • No set-off of brought-forward losses from earlier years under old regime
  • Must file Form 10-IC with the first income tax return — failure permanently forfeits the benefit for that year

MAT Does Not Apply — A Critical Advantage

Companies under Section 115BAB are not subject to Minimum Alternate Tax (MAT). This means even if the company has high book profits but low taxable income (due to accelerated depreciation or timing differences), it pays tax at 17.01% on taxable income — not 15% on book profit under MAT. This makes 115BAB significantly more attractive than the 22% regime for capital-intensive manufacturers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can a foreign company's Indian subsidiary claim the 15% tax rate under Section 115BAB?

A: Yes, if the Indian subsidiary is a domestic company (incorporated in India) that meets all 115BAB conditions: incorporated after October 1, 2019, commenced manufacturing before March 31, 2025, and engages only in manufacturing (no services, trading, or financial activities). A 100% foreign-owned WOS qualifies as a domestic company for this purpose.

Q: Can a company under 115BAB also claim PLI incentives?

A: Yes — 115BAB and PLI can be combined, and this is the optimal structure for new manufacturing MNCs. The 15% tax rate applies to all taxable income including PLI receipts. PLI incentives are taxable income, so the combined effective rate on PLI is 17.01% — still highly attractive given PLI subsidies of 4%–20% of incremental production value.

Q: What is the difference between the 22% rate under 115BAA and the 15% rate under 115BAB?

A: Section 115BAA (22% base rate, ~25.17% effective) is available to all existing domestic companies that forgo certain deductions. Section 115BAB (15% base rate, ~17.01% effective) is specifically for new domestic manufacturing companies incorporated after October 1, 2019. 115BAB has stricter eligibility — pure manufacturing only — but offers a significantly lower rate that is not available under 115BAA.
