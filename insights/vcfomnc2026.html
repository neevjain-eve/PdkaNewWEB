The MNC Subsidiary Finance Problem

Most foreign subsidiaries in India start with a small team and outsourced bookkeeping. That works at inception. But as revenue crosses Rs 5–10 crore, the compliance calendar multiplies: monthly GST returns, quarterly advance tax, annual TP documentation, FLA return to RBI, statutory audit, MCA filings, and group consolidation. Without senior financial oversight, missed deadlines and costly errors become routine — and parent company finance teams lose confidence in the India numbers.

When Does an MNC Subsidiary Need a Virtual CFO?

  • Revenue exceeds Rs 5 crore annually
  • Headcount exceeds 20 employees
  • International transactions exceed Rs 1 crore (mandatory TP documentation triggered)
  • Group audit requires component auditor coordination
  • Missed filing or RBI compounding situation has arisen
  • Parent finance team is asking questions India bookkeeper cannot answer

Cost Comparison: Full-Time CFO vs. Virtual CFO

Full-time CFO in Bangalore: Rs 60–150 lakh per year total compensation. The engagement is scalable: ramp up during audit season or year-end close, maintain at standard scope during steady state.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is a Virtual CFO and how is it different from a regular accountant?

A: A Virtual CFO is a senior finance professional who provides strategic financial leadership on a part-time or retainer basis. Unlike an accountant who records transactions, a Virtual CFO interprets financial results, manages compliance risk, advises on tax optimisation, coordinates audits, prepares investor reporting, and acts as the finance interface for the parent company — at a fraction of a full-time CFO's cost.
