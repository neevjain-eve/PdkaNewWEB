Overview

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) notified several amendments to the Companies Act, 2013 in 2025, aimed at easing compliance for small and medium enterprises while tightening governance for larger entities. Private limited companies must review their compliance calendars in light of these changes.

Revised Small Company Thresholds

Effective April 1, 2025, a company is classified as a small company if:

  • Paid-up share capital does not exceed ₹4 crore (previously ₹2 crore), AND
  • Turnover does not exceed ₹40 crore (previously ₹20 crore)

Small companies benefit from relaxed compliance — simplified board meeting requirements (2 meetings per year), abridged annual return (MGT-7A), and no mandatory rotation of auditors.

CSR Reporting — New Format

Companies with CSR obligations under Section 135 must now file the revised Form CSR-2 alongside their financial statements. Key additions include:

  • Activity-wise fund utilisation with GPS coordinates of project sites
  • Mandatory disclosure of implementing agency credentials
  • Impact assessment reports for CSR expenditure exceeding ₹1 crore per project

Related Party Transaction (RPT) Tightening

Amendments to Section 188 now require board approval (not just audit committee) for RPTs exceeding 10% of annual turnover or ₹100 crore, whichever is lower. Disclosure in the Board Report must include the business rationale and pricing methodology for each material RPT.

Director KYC & Disqualification

MCA has launched a continuous monitoring system for director disqualifications. Directors of companies that have failed to file financial statements for 3 consecutive years are automatically disqualified.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are the key Companies Act amendments that affect private limited companies in 2025-26?

A: Key 2025-26 changes include enhanced disclosure requirements for beneficial ownership, stricter CSR compliance for companies meeting the threshold (net profit Rs 5 crore+), updated CARO 2024 requirements for audit reporting, and MCA21 Version 3 portal mandatory for all filings. Bangalore-based private limited companies should specifically review their related-party transaction disclosure and board meeting minutes for these requirements.
