Karnataka — Technology, Aerospace & EV Hub

  • Capital Subsidy: 20%–30% of Fixed Capital Investment (FCI) in Zones C/D; 10%–15% in Zone B (Bangalore periphery)
  • Power Tariff: Exemption for 3–5 years for eligible new industries
  • Stamp Duty: 100% waiver in C/D zones; 50% in B zone
  • Best For: IT/ITES, GCCs, aerospace (HAL, ISRO ecosystem), electronics, EV batteries
  • Notable MNCs: Boeing, Airbus, Toyota, Samsung R&D, Intel, Goldman Sachs GCC

Tamil Nadu — Automotive & Electronics Manufacturing Belt

  • Capital Subsidy: Up to 25% of FCI under TIDCO Mega Industrial Scheme for investments above Rs 200 crore
  • Employment Subsidy: Rs 2,500–6,000 per employee per month for 5 years (100+ new direct jobs)
  • Power: Most reliable industrial power supply in southern India — 24/7 availability
  • Land: SIPCOT industrial parks at Sriperumbudur, Oragadam, Krishnagiri — serviced plots at concessional rates
  • Best For: Automotive (largest India cluster), electronics, pharma, FMCG
  • Notable MNCs: Hyundai, BMW, Foxconn, Samsung, Saint-Gobain

Telangana — Pharma, Life Sciences & IT

  • Policy: TS-iPASS — single-window clearance for all approvals within 15 working days
  • Capital Subsidy: 20%–25% of FCI for pharma and manufacturing in designated zones
  • Best For: Pharmaceuticals (40% of India's bulk drug production in Genome Valley), IT/ITES, biotech
  • Notable MNCs: Novartis, Dr Reddy's (JV), Microsoft, Google Hyderabad

Maharashtra — Finance, Automotive & Chemicals

  • Policy: Package Scheme of Incentives (PSI) 2019 — 20%–100% of state tax paid over 7–10 years depending on zone and investment
  • Stamp Duty: 100% waiver in D/D+ zones; 75% in C zone
  • Best For: Financial services, automotive, chemicals, FMCG, port-linked manufacturing (JNPT access)
  • Notable MNCs: Volkswagen, Bayer, P&G, Siemens, LG

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which Indian state offers the best incentives for electronics manufacturing MNCs?

A: Tamil Nadu and Karnataka are the top states for electronics manufacturing. Tamil Nadu offers strong port connectivity (Chennai), established supply chains (Foxconn, Samsung cluster at Sriperumbudur), and reliable power. Karnataka offers deeper tech talent and R&D ecosystem. Both offer 20%–25% capital subsidies and are PLI-eligible. Choice depends on supply chain proximity, port requirements, and talent profile needed.

Q: Which state is best for a Japanese company setting up auto component manufacturing in India?

A: Tamil Nadu (Chennai/Sriperumbudur) and Gujarat (Mandal-Becharaji, near Suzuki's base) are the top choices for Japanese auto component manufacturers. Tamil Nadu has the highest concentration of Japanese auto companies and a mature supply chain ecosystem. Gujarat offers strong logistics, Suzuki proximity, and state incentives including capital subsidies and power tariff support under the Vibrant Gujarat initiative.
