Why Transfer Pricing Matters More Than Ever

CBDT's transfer pricing (TP) assessments have intensified following India's adoption of BEPS Action Plans. Companies engaged in international transactions with associated enterprises (AEs) are now under heightened scrutiny, particularly in sectors like IT services, pharmaceuticals, and manufacturing.

Documentation Requirements Under Income Tax Act

Section 92D and Rule 10D mandate that every company with international transactions exceeding ₹1 crore maintain contemporaneous TP documentation. This includes:

  • Local File: Entity profile, functional analysis (FAR — Functions, Assets, Risks), industry overview, transaction descriptions, comparability analysis, and method selection
  • Master File (Form 3CEAA): Required for Indian constituent entities of MNE groups with consolidated revenue exceeding ₹500 crore or Indian aggregate value of international transactions exceeding ₹50 crore
  • CbCR (Country-by-Country Report): Applicable for Indian parent entities of MNE groups with consolidated revenue exceeding ₹5,500 crore

Most Scrutinised Transaction Types in 2025-26

  • Management fee charges from overseas parent companies — CBDT is challenging these where services are not demonstrably received
  • Software development services rendered to AEs — benchmarking of cost-plus margins under TNMM is closely examined
  • Guarantee commissions on loans extended by foreign holding companies
  • Secondment of employees from overseas entities — service PE risk and TP implications

Penalties for Non-Compliance

Failure to maintain or furnish TP documentation attracts:

  • 2% of transaction value for failure to maintain documentation
  • 2% of transaction value for failure to report international transactions
  • 100% to 300% of tax sought to be evaded if TP adjustment exceeds ₹1 crore
