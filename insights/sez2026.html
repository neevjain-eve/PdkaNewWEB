SEZ Tax Benefits in 2026: What Still Applies

  • Income Tax: Section 10AA — 100% exemption on export profits for Years 1–5, 50% for Years 6–10, 50% of reinvested profits for Years 11–15. Total: up to 15 years of phased tax benefit.
  • MAT: SEZ units are subject to 15% MAT from FY 2022-23 (full exemption withdrawn). MAT credit carried forward for 15 years.
  • GST: Supplies to SEZ units are zero-rated — suppliers claim ITC refunds, reducing input costs for the SEZ unit by 18%.
  • Customs: Duty-free import of capital goods, raw materials, consumables. No customs duty on goods from abroad or from domestic tariff area (DTA).
  • FDI: 100% FDI under automatic route for manufacturing and IT/ITES in SEZs.

How to Set Up in an SEZ: Step by Step

  1. Identify SEZ zone and developer (government or private)
  2. File Form F (Letter of Approval application) with Development Commissioner
  3. Receive Letter of Approval specifying permitted activity and net foreign exchange obligation
  4. Execute lease with SEZ developer
  5. Register with Customs as an SEZ unit; obtain Bond-cum-Legal Undertaking (BLUT)
  6. Maintain separate books of accounts for SEZ vs. any DTA operations
  7. File Annual Performance Report (APR) with Development Commissioner by April 30 each year

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can a foreign company's Indian subsidiary set up in an SEZ?

A: Yes. A 100% foreign-owned Indian subsidiary can set up as an SEZ unit and claim all SEZ benefits including the Section 10AA income tax exemption, duty-free imports, and zero-rated GST supplies. The subsidiary applies to the relevant SEZ's Development Commissioner for a Letter of Approval specifying the permitted manufacturing or service activity.
