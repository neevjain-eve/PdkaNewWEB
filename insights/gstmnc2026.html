GST Registration: Which Type Does Your Foreign Company Need?

  • Indian Subsidiary (WOS): Regular GST registration under the subsidiary's PAN. Full ITC entitlement. GSTR-1, GSTR-3B monthly. LUT for zero-rated exports.
  • No fixed establishment, making B2B supplies: Non-Resident Taxable Person (NRTP) registration — valid 90 days, renewable. Must deposit estimated tax in advance. No ITC available. GSTR-5 monthly.
  • Foreign digital services to Indian consumers (OIDAR): Mandatory registration regardless of turnover. File GSTR-5A quarterly.
  • Branch Office: Regular registration same as Indian company.

Letter of Undertaking (LUT) — The Most Important Filing for Service Exporters

Indian subsidiaries exporting services must file a fresh LUT on the GST portal before April 1 every year. Without LUT, the company must pay IGST on zero-rated exports and claim a refund — creating a 60–90 day cash flow gap that typically amounts to crores for mid-size IT companies.

GST Refund Process for Zero-Rated Export Services

  1. Accumulate ITC on inputs (software, cloud, office rent, hardware)
  2. File GSTR-1 declaring zero-rated export supplies
  3. File refund application in RFD-01 on GST portal with export invoices and FIRC/BRC
  4. CA certificate required for refunds exceeding Rs 2 lakh
  5. GST officer processes within 60 days; interest @6% if delayed beyond 60 days

ITC Reconciliation: The Hidden Cost of Poor GST Management

ITC can only be claimed on purchases reflected in GSTR-2B. For IT companies with many SaaS, cloud, and contractor vendors, GSTR-2B reconciliation is a monthly discipline. Vendors who don't file GSTR-1 block your ITC — forcing you to chase them or lose the credit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does a foreign company need to register for GST in India if it has no office there?

A: Yes, if the foreign company makes taxable supplies in India without a fixed establishment, it must register as a Non-Resident Taxable Person (NRTP) before making the first supply. OIDAR service providers supplying digital services to Indian consumers must also register regardless of having no India presence. Foreign companies with Indian subsidiaries — the subsidiary holds its own GST registration.

Q: What is a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) in GST?

A: A Letter of Undertaking (LUT) is a declaration filed on the GST portal by an exporter of goods or services, undertaking to export without payment of IGST and to comply with all GST export conditions. Filing LUT allows the exporter to supply zero-rated exports without paying IGST upfront, avoiding a cash flow burden. LUT must be filed annually before April 1 and is available to all registered exporters without a pending tax default.
