ITR Deadlines for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27)

  • July 31, 2026: ITR for individuals, HUFs, and firms not requiring audit
  • September 30, 2026: Tax Audit Report (Form 3CA/3CD) and Transfer Pricing Report (Form 3CEB)
  • October 31, 2026: ITR for companies, LLPs, and taxpayers requiring audit
  • November 30, 2026: ITR for taxpayers with international transactions (TP filing required)

New ITR Forms for AY 2026-27

  • ITR-6 (companies): New Schedule 115BAB for new manufacturing companies claiming the 15% tax rate. Enhanced disclosure on crypto asset transactions. Mandatory MSME vendor disclosure (payments outstanding beyond 45 days).
  • ITR-5 (LLPs/firms): Updated alternate minimum tax (AMT) computation. New fields for Partner remuneration above Section 40(b) limits.
  • Form 3CD (Tax Audit): Clause 44 now requires GST-wise breakup of all expenses — reconcile before filing. Clause 36A requires disclosure of deemed dividend under Section 2(22)(e).

Pre-Filing Compliance Checklist

  • Reconcile turnover with GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B — CBDT cross-matches these automatically
  • Verify TDS credits in Form 26AS and AIS (Annual Information Statement) match books
  • Ensure all advance tax installments paid — shortfall attracts 1% per month interest under 234B/234C
  • Confirm all related-party transactions are at arm's length and documented (Form 3CEB if international)
  • Check MSME payments — outstanding supplier dues beyond 45 days are disallowed under Section 43B(h)
  • Review depreciation schedules — WDV as per IT Act vs. books often diverges after Ind AS adoption

Common Errors That Trigger CBDT Scrutiny

  • Turnover mismatch between ITR and GSTR-1 (even Rs 1 difference triggers automated notice)
  • TDS deducted but not deposited on time — interest under 201(1A) and late filing fee under 234E
  • Claiming deduction under both Section 10AA (SEZ) and 115BAB (15% rate) — mutually exclusive
  • Cash payments above Rs 10,000 per day to a single party — disallowed under Section 40A(3)

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the due date for ITR filing for a Private Limited Company in India for FY 2025-26?

A: October 31, 2026 for companies requiring statutory audit. If the company has international transactions requiring a Transfer Pricing report (Form 3CEB), the deadline extends to November 30, 2026.

Q: What happens if a company misses the ITR filing deadline?

A: A belated return can be filed up to December 31, 2026 with a late fee of Rs 5,000 (Rs 1,000 for small taxpayers). Missing the deadline also means the company loses the right to carry forward business losses (other than depreciation) to future years — a significant cost for loss-making companies.

Q: Does a foreign company's Indian subsidiary need to file ITR in India?

A: Yes. An Indian subsidiary is a domestic company and must file ITR-6 by October 31 (or November 30 if transfer pricing applies). Even if the subsidiary made no profit, a nil return must be filed to maintain good standing with CBDT and to preserve the right to carry forward losses.
