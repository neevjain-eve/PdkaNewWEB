GST Compliance Calendar for Bangalore Businesses

  • GSTR-1: Monthly (11th of next month) or Quarterly (13th of month after quarter end) — outward supply details
  • GSTR-3B: Monthly self-assessed return — GST payment and input credit utilisation
  • GSTR-9: Annual return by December 31
  • GSTR-9C: Annual reconciliation statement certified by CA — mandatory for turnover above Rs 5 crore
  • LUT Filing: Annual Letter of Undertaking renewal (April 1) for zero-rated exports
  • GSTR-7: Monthly return for TDS deductors (government entities)

GST Issues Specific to Bangalore Tech Companies

  • Software exports: IT services to foreign clients — zero-rated supply under IGST Act; must maintain proof of foreign currency receipt and FIRC/BRC
  • Mixed supplies: SaaS companies with both domestic and international customers must maintain meticulous place-of-supply records
  • ITC on office expenses: Blocked credit under Section 17(5) for motor vehicles, food, club memberships — common IT expense categories
  • IGST refunds on exports: Companies exporting services can claim IGST refunds on all eligible input services and capital goods — PDKA has recovered multi-crore GST refunds for IT clients
  • GST on seconded employees from foreign parent: Provision of employees by foreign parent may be treated as import of services — RCM applicable

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is GST applicable on software exports from India?

A: Software services exported from India to foreign clients are treated as zero-rated supplies under the IGST Act — GST is not charged, and the exporter can claim a refund of all input GST paid on inputs used to deliver those exports. The key condition is that the place of supply is outside India and payment is received in foreign currency. Zero-rating requires filing a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) each April — without an LUT, export GST must be paid upfront and then refunded.

Q: Can a Bangalore IT company claim GST refund on office rent paid for an SEZ client's project?

A: If the IT company is providing services to a customer in an SEZ, those services are zero-rated (IGST Act, Section 16). The IT company can claim a refund of ITC on inputs attributable to those zero-rated supplies. However, the apportionment between zero-rated (SEZ and export) and taxable (domestic) supplies must be calculated per Rule 89 of the CGST Rules.
