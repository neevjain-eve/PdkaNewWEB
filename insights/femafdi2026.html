The Five FEMA Filings Every Foreign Company Must Know

1. FC-GPR — Share Allotment to Foreign Investor

File within 30 days of allotting shares to a foreign investor via FIRMS portal. Required documents: allotment letter, CA/Merchant Banker valuation certificate (DCF method), KYC of investor. Late filing triggers compounding.

2. FLA Return — Annual Foreign Liabilities and Assets

File by July 15 each year for any company with outstanding FDI or overseas investment in its balance sheet. Based on audited March 31 financials. Late filing penalty: Rs 10,000 + Rs 2,000 per day. Discovered during first statutory audit by many companies — often already in default by then.

3. FC-TRS — Transfer of Shares

File within 60 days when non-resident buys from resident (or vice versa). Covers secondary sales, buybacks, and exits. Valuation certificate from CA mandatory.

4. ODI — Overseas Investment by Indian Entity

File Form ODI within 30 days when the Indian subsidiary invests in or lends to a foreign entity. Annual Performance Report (APR) filed by July 31 covering all overseas investments made.

5. SOFTEX — IT Export Certification

File within 30 days of each export invoice for software/IT services. STPI-registered companies file online; others through AD bank. Most commonly missed filing for Bangalore IT companies.

RBI Compounding — Regularising Past Violations

Companies that missed past FEMA filings can regularise via compounding under Section 15 of FEMA. Application filed with RBI Compounding Authority disclosing all contraventions. RBI levies a fee (typically 0.5%–1% of contravention amount) and issues a compounding order that closes the matter permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is FC-GPR and when must it be filed?

A: Form FC-GPR (Foreign Currency — Gross Provisional Return) must be filed by an Indian company with the RBI through the FIRMS portal within 30 days of allotting shares to a foreign investor. It reports the amount of FDI received, shares allotted, valuation, and investor KYC. Late filing requires a compounding application with RBI.

Q: What is the FLA Return and who must file it?

A: The Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA) must be filed with RBI by July 15 every year by any Indian company that has received FDI or made overseas investments (ODI). It captures outstanding balances as of March 31. Even companies that received FDI years ago must continue filing FLA as long as the investment remains outstanding.

Q: Can FEMA violations be regularised? What is the compounding process?

A: Yes. FEMA violations can be regularised through the compounding process under Section 15 of FEMA. The company files a compounding application with the RBI Compounding Authority disclosing all contraventions. RBI issues a compounding order levying a monetary penalty (typically 0.5%–1% of contravention amount). Once paid, the violation is permanently closed and does not affect future RBI dealings.
