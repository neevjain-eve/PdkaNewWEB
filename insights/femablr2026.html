Common FEMA Violations at Bangalore Subsidiaries

  • FC-GPR not filed within 30 days of share allotment to foreign investor
  • FLA Return not filed by July 15 despite having outstanding FDI
  • Valuation not done by SEBI-registered merchant banker or CA for preference share issuance
  • Overseas Direct Investment (ODI) forms not filed before investing in foreign subsidiary
  • ECB (External Commercial Borrowing) loan not reported within 7 days of drawdown
  • FEMA pricing guidelines violated on purchase/sale of shares between residents and non-residents

FEMA Compounding: Correcting Past Violations

FEMA compounding allows companies to voluntarily disclose and pay a penalty to regularise past violations — avoiding prosecution. The RBI calculates compounding amounts based on contravention amount and period. PDKA handles the entire compounding process: drafting the application, calculating the contravention amount, coordinating with RBI's compounding authority, and obtaining the compounding order. Most violations can be compounded in 6–18 months.

FLA Return — The Most Missed Filing

Every Indian company that has received FDI or made overseas investments must file the Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA Return) with RBI by July 15. This is an online return filed on the RBI FLAIR portal. Required data: FDI outstanding as of March 31, equity and debt flows during the year, and fair value of investments. PDKA prepares and files FLA Returns for all subsidiary clients as part of the annual compliance package.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is FC-GPR and when must it be filed?

A: FC-GPR (Foreign Currency - Gross Provisional Return) is an RBI reporting form that must be filed within 30 days of allotting shares to a foreign investor. It reports the amount received, number of shares allotted, valuation methodology, and shareholder details. Late FC-GPR filing is a FEMA violation requiring compounding. PDKA prepares FC-GPR filings for all client FDI transactions and tracks the 30-day deadline from share allotment.

Q: What is the penalty for FEMA violations in India?

A: FEMA violations attract a compounding penalty typically calculated at a fixed amount plus 0.5%–1% of the contravention amount per year. The specific formula depends on the nature of the violation. For FC-GPR delays, RBI typically charges a fixed penalty of Rs 5,000–Rs 50,000 plus the formula amount. Voluntary compounding (before RBI initiates action) generally results in lower penalties than enforcement proceedings. PDKA advises on compounding strategy to minimise penalties.
