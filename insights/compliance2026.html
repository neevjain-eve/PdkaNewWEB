Why MNC Subsidiaries Miss Deadlines — and What It Costs

India's compliance calendar spans five regulators — RBI, MCA, CBDT, GST Council, and SEBI — each with its own portal, form, and penalty regime. A missed GST return attracts 18% interest per annum. A missed RBI filing (FLA, FC-GPR) requires compounding at 0.5%–1% of the contravention amount. A missed income tax return loses the right to carry forward losses. Here is the complete month-by-month calendar.

Q1 (April–June)

  • April 1: File new LUT for zero-rated GST exports (annual renewal)
  • April 15: TDS payment for March quarter
  • May 31: TDS returns Q4 — Form 26Q (domestic) and 27Q (foreign payments)
  • June 15: First advance tax installment — 15% of annual estimated tax

Q2 (July–September)

  • July 15: FLA Return to RBI — Annual Return on Foreign Liabilities and Assets
  • July 31: TDS returns Q1; ITR for non-audit cases
  • September 15: Second advance tax installment — 45% cumulative
  • September 30: Tax Audit Report (3CA/3CD), Transfer Pricing Report (Form 3CEB), Master File (Form 3CEAA)

Q3 (October–December)

  • October 31: ITR-6 for companies; MCA Annual Return (MGT-7A); Financial Statements (AOC-4)
  • November 30: CbCR notification (Form 3CEAC); CbCR filing (Form 3CEAD) if Indian parent entity
  • December 15: Third advance tax installment — 75% cumulative
  • December 31: GSTR-9 Annual Return and GSTR-9C Reconciliation Statement

Q4 (January–March)

  • January 31: TDS returns Q3; FC-GPR for Q3 share allotments
  • February/March: Statutory audit fieldwork typically completed; draft financials circulated
  • March 15: Final advance tax installment — 100% cumulative
  • March 31: Pay any pending RBI compounding fees; close any open FEMA regularisation matters

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: When must a company file its transfer pricing report in India?

A: Form 3CEB (Transfer Pricing Accountant's Report) must be filed by September 30 of the assessment year — i.e., September 30, 2026 for FY 2025-26. Form 3CEAA (Master File) is due on the same date. The income tax return for companies with TP transactions (November 30 deadline) cannot be filed without these forms being submitted first.

Q: What happens if an Indian company misses the MCA Annual Return filing?

A: The MCA Annual Return (MGT-7A for small companies, MGT-7 for others) must be filed within 60 days of the Annual General Meeting (AGM), which must be held by September 30 — making the effective deadline November 30. Late filing attracts additional fees of Rs 100 per day of delay for each form. Directors who are in default also face personal liability under the Companies Act 2013.
