Why the Entry Structure Decision Is Irreversible in the Short Term

The legal structure through which a foreign company enters India determines its tax rate, repatriation rights, eligible government incentives, and regulatory burden for the next 5–10 years. Changing structure later — e.g., converting a Branch Office to a Subsidiary — requires fresh RBI approvals, tax clearances, and asset transfers that can take 12–18 months. Getting it right at the start is essential.

Option 1: Wholly Owned Subsidiary (Private Limited Company)

The default choice for 90%+ of foreign companies entering India. 100% FDI permitted under automatic route for most sectors. Taxed at 22% (existing) or 15% (new manufacturing — Section 115BAB). Full operational freedom: hire employees, bid for government contracts, sign client agreements, own property. Eligible for PLI subsidies and SEZ benefits. Dividends repatriable after 20% withholding tax (lower under DTAA). Compliance: statutory audit, MCA filings, GST, TDS, income tax, FEMA.

Option 2: Branch Office

Requires prior RBI approval. Permitted for: export/import, professional services, R&D, technical collaboration. Prohibited for: retail trading, manufacturing (except SEZ). Taxed at 40% — significantly less efficient than a subsidiary. Net profits repatriable after tax. Annual Activity Certificate to RBI mandatory. Best for: companies testing India before full commitment, or those in consulting with limited headcount needs.

Option 3: Liaison Office

Zero revenue permitted — purely representational. Market research, promoting parent's products, coordinating with Indian partners only. All expenses funded by parent remittances. No tax filing required unless PE risk arises. Annual Activity Certificate to RBI. Best for: pre-entry exploration phase only.

Sector-Specific FDI Caps to Know

  • Defence: 74% automatic, beyond that Government route
  • Insurance: 74% automatic
  • Broadcasting: 49% (news), 100% (non-news) with Government approval
  • Multi-brand retail: 51% Government route with conditions
  • Most others (IT, manufacturing, consulting, pharma, auto): 100% automatic route

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best way for a foreign company to enter India in 2026?

A: For most foreign companies, a Wholly Owned Subsidiary (Private Limited Company) is the optimal structure. It provides 100% operational control, access to the 15% manufacturing tax rate under Section 115BAB, eligibility for PLI subsidies, and the ability to hire locally and bid for government contracts. A Branch Office is second choice for limited-scope operations; a Liaison Office only for pre-entry market research.

Q: What is the minimum capital requirement to set up a company in India?

A: There is no minimum paid-up capital requirement for a Private Limited Company in India under the Companies Act 2013. However, the FDI amount remitted by the foreign parent must align with the valuation certified by a CA or SEBI-registered Merchant Banker at the time of share allotment for FC-GPR filing.
