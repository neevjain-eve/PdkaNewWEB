What Is the PLI Scheme?

India's Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme offers cash subsidies of 4%–20% of incremental production value to companies that manufacture in India. Total government outlay: over Rs 2 lakh crore across 14 sectors. MNCs are fully eligible through their Indian WOS. PLI is a performance-linked annual payment — not an upfront grant — disbursed after verified production.

PLI Sectors and Incentive Rates (2026)

  • Mobile Phones and Electronics: 4%–6% on incremental sales
  • Pharmaceuticals (KSM/API): 10%–20% based on production method
  • Automobiles and Auto Components: up to 18% for advanced automotive products
  • Advanced Chemistry Cells (batteries): 18% per GWh capacity
  • Semiconductors and Display Fabs: 50% of capital expenditure — largest single incentive globally
  • Solar PV Modules: 4%–6%
  • Telecom Products: 6%
  • White Goods (AC, LED): 6%
  • Speciality Steel: 4%–6%
  • Textile (MMF and technical): up to 15%
  • Food Processing: 10%
  • Medical Devices: 5%

How to Claim PLI: Step by Step

  1. Submit application to relevant ministry during the open window
  2. Ministry determines baseline production (typically FY 2019-20)
  3. Manufacture and sell above baseline in Year 1
  4. File annual claim with CA-certified incremental sales certificate
  5. Ministry site inspection and verification
  6. Incentive disbursed to bank account within 60 days of approval

Combining PLI With 115BAB and State Subsidies

The most tax-efficient structure for MNC manufacturers: new WOS incorporated after Oct 2019 qualifies for 15% corporate tax (Section 115BAB) + PLI cash subsidy (4%–20%) + state capital subsidy (5%–25%) + power tariff concession.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can a foreign-owned Indian company apply for PLI scheme benefits?

A: Yes. A 100% foreign-owned Indian subsidiary (WOS) is fully eligible for PLI scheme benefits. The scheme requires Indian incorporation — a Branch Office or foreign company cannot apply directly. The subsidiary must meet the minimum investment threshold and domestic value addition requirements of the specific PLI scheme it applies under.

Q: What is the PLI incentive for electronics manufacturing in India?

A: The PLI scheme for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing offers 4%–6% of incremental sales for mobile phones and specified electronic components over 5 years. For mobile phones, companies targeting global exports get a 6% incentive. The scheme has already attracted Apple (through Foxconn, Wistron, Pegatron), Samsung, and Dixon Technologies.

Q: Is PLI income taxable in India?

A: PLI incentive receipts are taxable as business income under the Income Tax Act. However, since PLI-eligible companies typically qualify for the 15% tax rate under Section 115BAB, the effective tax on PLI income is 17.01% (including surcharge and cess). PLI incentives are not capital receipts and cannot be claimed as tax-exempt.
