Why Japanese Investment in India Is Accelerating

Japan is among India's top 5 FDI source countries with cumulative investment exceeding USD 40 billion. Drivers in 2026: China+1 supply chain diversification, India-Japan CEPA, JBIC and JETRO support programs, and India's PLI scheme targeting sectors where Japanese companies lead (auto components, electronics, pharma). India-Japan DTAA: Key Treaty Benefits

  • Dividends: 10% (if 25%+ shareholding) or 15% — vs. domestic 20%
  • Interest: 10% — vs. domestic 20%
  • Royalties: 10% — vs. domestic 20%
  • Fees for Technical Services: 10% — vs. domestic 20%
  • Capital Gains: Taxable in India for land-rich companies; Japan residents claim treaty protection for portfolio investments

Japanese Investment Zones in India

  • Neemrana (Rajasthan): 60+ Japanese companies; part of Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor; dedicated Japanese-language industrial services
  • Sriperumbudur (Tamil Nadu): Preferred auto and electronics cluster for Japanese companies near Chennai port
  • Mandal-Becharaji (Gujarat): Suzuki/Maruti base; strong Japanese automotive ecosystem
  • Tumkur (Karnataka): Japanese industrial park near Bangalore; precision components and electronics

Key Japan-India Compliance Considerations

  • Financial year alignment: India uses April–March; Japan also uses April–March — simplifies group reporting, but India results need Ind AS to JGAAP conversion
  • CFC rules: Japan's CFC (Controlled Foreign Corporation) rules may tax undistributed profits of the Indian subsidiary in Japan if the effective Indian tax rate is below a threshold.
  • Social security: India-Japan have no totalization agreement — Japanese secondees in India pay both Indian PF contributions and Japanese social security. This double burden needs to be factored into secondee cost planning.
  • TP for auto components: CBDT specifically scrutinises Japanese auto component companies with India manufacturing — internal sales to Indian assembly units and intercompany pricing on tooling and dies are common TP disputes

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can a Japanese company set up a manufacturing unit in India without a local Indian partner?

A: Yes. India permits 100% FDI under the automatic route for most manufacturing sectors. Japanese companies can own 100% of their Indian manufacturing subsidiary without an Indian joint venture partner. However, many Japanese companies prefer JV structures for market knowledge, local distribution networks, and government relationship management — particularly in defence and regulated sectors.
