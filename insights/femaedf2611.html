What changed

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) notified the Foreign Exchange Management (Export and Import of Goods and Services) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026 (Notification No. FEMA 23(R)/(2)/2026-RB, dated 2 November 2026, published in the Gazette the same day). It amends the Export and Import of Goods and Services Regulations, 2026, which came into force on 1 October 2026. The consolidated text on the RBI website shows that the amendments to the provisions described below take effect from 1 October 2026. The RBI listed a press release on the amendment on 20 November 2026.

Who is affected

Exporters of services, importers of services, and the Authorised Dealer (AD) banks that handle their payments.

Key provisions

  • Export Declaration Form (EDF) for services, resident individuals (Regulation 3(2)(a)): a service exporter who is a resident individual is deemed to have submitted the EDF when the exporter gives the AD the information needed in relation to the receipt of payment, for crediting the exporter's account.
  • EDF for services, other exporters (Regulation 3(2)(b)): an exporter who is a person other than a resident individual is deemed to have submitted the EDF in the same way, but only if the value of the inward remittance for the service is not more than ₹10 lakh (or its equivalent in foreign currency).
  • Import of services (Regulation 18(1)(d)): the provision on entering details of service imports in the Import Data Processing and Monitoring System (IDPMS) was substituted. As it now reads, it applies to an importer who is a person other than a resident individual where the invoice or contract value is more than ₹10 lakh (or its equivalent in foreign currency), and the AD is to make the entry within five working days of receipt of details. The earlier wording referred to "documents" instead of "details".
  • Other changes: a definition of "resident individual" was inserted in Regulation 2(1), (clause (da)), and the existing provisos to Regulation 3(2) were renumbered as (c), (d) and (e).

Key dates

  • 1 October 2026: date from which the amended provisions take effect, as shown in the consolidated text.
  • 2 November 2026: date of the Second Amendment notification and its Gazette publication.
  • 20 November 2026: date of the RBI press release listing.

Practical points

  • The ₹10 lakh figure in Regulation 3(2)(b) is the value of the inward remittance for the service. It is not described as a limit on the EDF amount.
  • Because the amended provisions are stated to take effect from 1 October 2026, a date before the notification, service transactions since that date may be read against the amended text.
  • The other EDF timing rules for service exporters in Regulation 3(2), including the 30-day filing period, continue to appear in the consolidated text.
  • The consolidated text on the RBI website was used for this summary. The notification as published in the Gazette, together with any RBI Directions, is the authoritative text and should be read for any particular transaction.
