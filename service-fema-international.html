FEMA & International

FEMA compliance and business entry advisory for foreign companies entering India.

Overview

With clients across 9 countries, PDKA has deep expertise in FEMA regulations, cross-border transactions, and business entry strategies for foreign entities. We advise MNCs, start-ups with international shareholders, and Indian companies with overseas investments.

What We Do

FEMA Compliance

Advisory on FDI, ODI, ECB, LRS, and other foreign exchange transactions. Includes FC-GPR, FC-TRS, FLA returns and RBI filings.

Business Entry into India

End-to-end support for foreign companies setting up in India — liaison office, branch office, wholly owned subsidiary, joint venture structuring.

Transfer Pricing

Documentation, benchmarking analysis and Form 3CEB certification for international transactions between associated enterprises.

DTAA Advisory

Advisory on Double Tax Avoidance Agreements to optimise withholding tax on payments to non-residents. Covers royalties, fees for technical services, dividends and interest.

Foreign Remittances (Form 15CA/CB)

Certification of Form 15CA/CB for all remittances to non-residents under the Income Tax Act and FEMA.

Overseas Direct Investment (ODI)

Guidance and filing support for Indian companies investing in overseas subsidiaries or joint ventures including APR filing.
