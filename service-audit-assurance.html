Audit & Assurance

Independent, rigorous audit services in full compliance with ICAI Standards.

Overview

PDKA provides comprehensive statutory and internal audit services to companies, LLPs, trusts, and non-profit organisations. Our audit methodology is built on SA (Standards on Auditing) issued by ICAI, ensuring independence, objectivity, and professional scepticism at every stage.

What We Do

Statutory Audit

Mandatory audit under the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable laws. Includes balance sheet, P&L, cash flow and notes to accounts verification.

Internal Audit

Risk-based internal audit covering financial controls, operational processes, procurement, payroll, and IT systems.

Tax Audit (Sec 44AB)

Audit and certification of accounts under the Income Tax Act, 1961 for businesses and professionals crossing prescribed turnover thresholds.

GST Audit & Review

Reconciliation of books with GST returns, ITC review, and identification of compliance gaps.

Bank Audit

Concurrent and statutory bank branch audits with focus on NPA identification, KYC norms, and credit appraisal.

Trust & NGO Audit

Audit of charitable institutions, trusts, and NGOs with specific reporting for 12A/80G compliance.
