IS Audit (CISA/DISA)

Globally certified IS Audit by CISA and DISA qualified professionals.

Overview

PDKA is one of the few CA firms offering dedicated Information Systems Audit services led by professionals holding both CISA (Certified Information Systems Auditor — ISACA) and DISA (Diploma in Information Systems Audit — ICAI). We assess IT controls, cyber risks, and data integrity across your technology landscape.

What We Do

IT General Controls (ITGC) Audit

Review of access controls, change management, IT operations, and backup/recovery procedures. Essential for SOX, SOC 2, and ICAI standards compliance.

ERP Audit (SAP / Oracle / Tally / Zoho)

Audit of ERP configurations, user access rights, financial module controls, and data integrity checks.

Cyber Security Review

Assessment of network security, endpoint protection, email security, data loss prevention (DLP) and incident response readiness.

Data Privacy & DPDPA Compliance

Gap assessment for compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — consent management, breach response, data principal rights.

IS Audit for Statutory Audit

CISA/DISA-led IS audit component integrated into statutory audit to assess IT-dependent controls and automated processes.

Cloud Security Review

Review of cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP), IAM controls, data residency, and third-party access management.
